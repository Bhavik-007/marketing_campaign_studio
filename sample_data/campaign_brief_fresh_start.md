# Fresh Start oral care campaign

## Campaign objective

Encourage adults to refresh their everyday oral-care routine at the start of summer. The campaign should introduce the Fresh Start toothpaste range and invite customers to learn about the available products.

## Audience

- Adults aged 25–45 who purchase personal-care products online
- Existing customers who have consented to marketing communications
- Priority segments: retail and enterprise customer programmes

## Product facts

- Fresh Start toothpaste is available in fluoride and sensitive-care variants.
- The fluoride variant is intended for everyday brushing.
- The sensitive-care variant is designed for customers who prefer a gentle option.
- Products are available through participating stores and the company product catalogue.

## Required message

Summer is a useful moment to review everyday oral-care habits. Customers can explore the Fresh Start range to choose a toothpaste that suits their routine.

## Creative direction

- Clean, bright bathroom setting with a fresh morning feel
- White, aqua, and soft mint colour palette
- Premium but approachable commercial photography
- Show a toothpaste tube and a simple toothbrush; avoid medical imagery

## Email guidance

- Tone: helpful, clear, and optimistic
- Do not claim clinical outcomes, discounts, limited-time pricing, or guarantees.
- Use a neutral call to action: “Explore the Fresh Start range.”
- Do not invent URLs, dates, product features, or endorsements.
