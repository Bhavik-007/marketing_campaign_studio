Campaign test upload

1. Open the Brief tab in Marketing Campaign Studio.
2. Upload campaign_brief_fresh_start.md and campaign_product_facts.csv together.
3. Click Prepare campaign brief.
4. Optionally save and search the campaign in Elasticsearch.
5. Open Creative studio to generate an image prompt without uploading a reference image.
6. Open Email review to generate a customer-ready draft.

All product claims and campaign constraints for this test are contained in the uploaded files.
